Price data

Card images are built in: matching TCGplayer product images are used where available, with high-resolution card scans as fallbacks. Images, prices and cases can be changed in the Admin panel.

Prices are a built-in snapshot estimated from eBay and TCGplayer sold listings and PSA auction records, not live data. Gem chance is the probability a pull of that card comes out as a PSA 10.

How the math works: each card's drop weight is 1 ÷ raw price0.9, so a $10,000 card is roughly 5,000 times rarer than a $2 card. Then a second roll decides whether the pull is a PSA 10 (the gem chance). Case price is set so the expected value returns 88% of what you pay (adjustable in Admin), similar to real case openers.
